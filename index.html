02 / About

Built for the ride, not the race.

Long Beach Bike Club is a local club for people who like riding together around Long Beach, NY. It is social first: show up, roll out, and meet your neighbors along the way. Everything starts in our private Facebook group.

M-01Social

Ride together

Group rides built around conversation and a good pace for the people in them.

M-02Local

Know the area

Boardwalk, bay side and back streets. Local riders swap routes and favorite stops.

M-03Open

Everyone rolls

Neighbors of every pace and bike are welcome. Bring a helmet and a friend.

  • R-01Check the groupRides are announced in the Facebook group with time and meeting point.
  • R-02Show up readyHelmet on, tires pumped, water bottle full. Lights if it is getting late.
  • R-03Ride socialKeep it friendly, look out for each other, and follow the rules of the road.

05 / Locations

Where we roll.

Home is Long Beach, NY. From there the ride area stretches east to Point Lookout, west along the Rockaway boardwalk, and north over the bridge to the Five Towns.

L-01Island

Point Lookout

The eastern tip of the barrier island, where the ocean meets Jones Inlet. A small beach hamlet with a laid-back, end-of-the-road feel.

L-02Island

Lido Beach

A quiet residential stretch between Point Lookout and Long Beach, home to Lido Beach Town Park and wide-open ocean views.

L-03Island

Long Beach

Our home base. A city on the barrier island with a boardwalk of about 2.2 miles, ocean beaches and a walkable downtown.

L-04Island

Atlantic Beach

The western end of the island, a village with beach clubs and a bridge link to the mainland and the Five Towns.

L-05Rockaways

Rockaway Beach

Across the Nassau line in Queens. A long boardwalk, surf beaches and a lively food scene make it a favorite ride destination.

L-06Rockaways

Arverne

A Rockaway neighborhood along the ocean with beachfront and the boardwalk, between Rockaway Beach and Far Rockaway.

L-07Five Towns

Lawrence

A residential village on the mainland side, reached from the island by bridge. Quiet streets and shaded roads.

L-08Five Towns

Cedarhurst

A village with a busy Central Avenue shopping and dining strip, good for a coffee stop on the way back.
