05 / Locations

Where we ride.

Home is Long Beach. We roll east to Point Lookout, west along the Rockaway boardwalk, and north over the bridge to the Five Towns.

L-01Island

Point Lookout

The eastern tip of the barrier island, where the ocean meets Jones Inlet. A small, laid-back beach hamlet.

L-02Island

Lido Beach

A quiet residential stretch between Point Lookout and Long Beach, with Lido Beach Town Park and open ocean views.

L-03Island

Long Beach

Our home base. A barrier-island city with a boardwalk of about 2.2 miles, ocean beaches and a walkable downtown.

L-04Island

Atlantic Beach

The western end of the island. Beach clubs, plus a bridge to the mainland and the Five Towns.

L-05Rockaways

Rockaway Beach

Across the line in Queens. A long boardwalk, surf beaches and a lively food scene.

L-06Rockaways

Arverne

A Rockaway neighborhood on the ocean, between Rockaway Beach and Far Rockaway.

L-07Five Towns

Lawrence

A residential village on the mainland side, reached from the island by bridge. Quiet, shaded streets.

L-08Five Towns

Cedarhurst

A village with a busy Central Avenue shopping and dining strip. A good coffee stop on the way back.
